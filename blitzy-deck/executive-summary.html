Why It Matters

The value is reproducibility, regression safety and reach — none of which the product had before.

Business value

What The Business Gets

  • A run replays exactly from its seed; a defect travels as one number.
  • Five automated gates replace review by eye.
  • The board is reachable by keyboard and screen reader.
  • Hosting stays free and static.

Accessibility No language or screen-reader markup, and three controls no keyboard could reach Labelled, focusable and announced
Page contract Eight lookups into the page, none of them checked Guarded, so a rename is no longer fatal
Hosting cost Free static host Unchanged, and protected by mandate

Risks And Mitigations

Five risks were taken knowingly. Each one ships with the thing that answers it.

Risk register

What Could Go Wrong, And What Answers It

Accepted risks and their mitigations
Risk Mitigation
Older browsers lose support: three compatibility shims are gone and hardware-accelerated rendering is now required, which drops a long tail of legacy devices. A number-only mode ships as a first-class, fully playable path, not a degraded fallback.
Development no longer starts by double-clicking the page, because module-based delivery needs a served origin. One documented command serves the built bundle locally, and the deployment story is unchanged.
Rendering now depends on graphics support being present on the player's machine. A capability probe at startup, a handler for a lost rendering context, and an automatic switch to the fallback renderer.
The recorded gameplay proof could silently capture a black screen, passing a file-exists check while proving nothing. Verified software-rendering flags, plus an assertion on real rendered content rather than on duration alone.
The project's historical design freeze is deliberately superseded by this work. The governing contribution guide is updated, and the original palette, colour ramp and animation timings remain the default theme.

Every decision behind these, with the alternatives weighed and the risk carried, is recorded in docs/DECISION_LOG.md
